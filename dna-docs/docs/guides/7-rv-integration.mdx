## Requirements

RV must be running on the same machine as your browser with its network protocol enabled (in RV's Network dialog), and Enable RV Sync must be on in DNA's settings. DNA scans local ports for open RV sessions, so no manual port configuration is needed.

## Automated In-Review

Open the RV Sync menu in the sidebar and connect to your RV session. As you move through the playlist in RV, DNA follows the playhead and sets the matching version to in-review automatically. The button reads "RV Live" while connected, and the menu shows which version is currently in review. If the connection drops, reopen the menu to scan and reconnect.

## Pinning a Version In-Review

To hold a version in review while RV keeps playing, use the pin toggle on its version card. The card shows a Pinned label, and RV sync leaves the pinned version alone even as the playhead moves. Unpin it to resume following RV, and in-review snaps back to whatever is under the playhead.
