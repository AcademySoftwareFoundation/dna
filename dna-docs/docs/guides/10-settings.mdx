## Settings Panel

<Figure
  src="img/10-settings/settings-modal.png"
  width="75%"
  alt="DNA settings"
  caption="Image shows DNA settings modal."
/>

The settings panel is where you can shape DNA to your workflow: appearance (light or dark), the In-Review and RV Sync toggles, production-tracking tab sync, keyboard shortcuts, and all AI features including note generation, prompts, glossaries, and Note QC. In the Keybindings tab, click any shortcut to record a new key combination, press Escape to cancel, or reset everything to defaults. Some toggles can be locked by your pipeline's configuration; hover over them to see why.
