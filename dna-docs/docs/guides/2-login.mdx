## Signing in

How you sign in depends on how your studio configured DNA.

| Mode | What you enter |
| --- | --- |
| Development (`none`) | Email associated with your Flow / ShotGrid user |
| Google | Google account used by your studio |
| ShotGrid / Flow (`shotgrid`) | Flow **legacy Login** and **legacy password** |

For ShotGrid auth, complete the one-time
[Flow Account Setup](/docs/2-account-setup) first (Autodesk PAT bound in Flow,
plus Legacy Login enabled). After that, use those credentials on the DNA
sign-in form — not your Autodesk Account password, and not the PAT string.

Once you are signed in, choose a project and playlist to open.
