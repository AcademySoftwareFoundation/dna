## Signing In with Flow Production Tracking

Sign in with your Flow Production Tracking username and password. DNA checks
them against your studio's site, then shows a dropdown list of projects and
playlists to choose from. Everything you do in DNA, such as publishing notes,
is done as you in Flow Production Tracking.

You stay signed in for 12 hours by default; after that DNA asks you to sign
in again. In local development the backend accepts any password.
