## Joining a Call

Open the Transcription menu in the sidebar, paste a Google Meet or Microsoft Teams meeting URL (plus a passcode if required), and click Start Transcription. A bot joins the call and transcript segments stream into the transcript panel in real time, attached to whichever version is in review.

<Figure
  src="img/5-transcription/join-call.png"
  width="75%"
  alt="DNA transcription"
  caption="Image shows how to join a call."
/>

Once connected, you can pause transcription at any time, during a break for example. The bot stays in the meeting, but nothing is saved until you resume. When the session wraps, click Stop Transcription to remove the bot from the call.
