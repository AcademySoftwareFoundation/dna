With the DNA plugin for Hiero, DNA stays on the version under the playhead in Hiero. The version [in review](./4-in-review.mdx) changes as you move through a timeline, so notes and transcript segments land on the right version without anyone setting it by hand. It works with both Hiero and Nuke Studio.

:::info
If Hiero has no **DNA** menu in its menu bar, the plugin has not been installed yet. If you need to install it yourself, follow [Installing the Plugin](/integrations/hiero#installing-the-plugin) in the developer docs.
:::

## Sign In From Hiero

1. In Hiero, choose **DNA > Sign In...**. The first time, Hiero asks for your DNA URL, which is the address you open DNA at in your browser.
2. Sign in, then choose your show and the playlist you are reviewing.
3. If your show has DNA sessions, Hiero asks which session to drive.
4. The DNA menu shows **Signed in as** with your email and the session, and the playlist you chose.

The sign-in window depends on your version of Hiero. Some open DNA's own sign-in page inside Hiero. Others, including Nuke Studio 17, show a short form that asks for your email instead, which only works where DNA uses email sign-in.

Hiero remembers this when you quit, so you only sign in once.

## Review

1. In DNA, open the same session and switch on **Follow In Review** at the top of the sidebar.
2. In Hiero, open the timeline and review as usual.

Each time the playhead moves onto another version, DNA sets it in review and jumps to it. Everyone else with the playlist open sees the in-review marker move too.

Hiero only sets versions that are on your playlist. With a session chosen, it looks through all of the session's playlists. Other clips on the timeline are ignored.

If someone has [pinned](./4-in-review.mdx#pinning) a version in DNA, it stays in review until it is unpinned, whatever Hiero is playing.

If DNA misses a change, choose **DNA > Send current version now** to send the clip under the playhead again.

## Change Playlist, Session or Sign Out

- To review a different playlist, choose **DNA > Switch show/playlist...**. Hiero does not follow playlists you open elsewhere.
- To drive a different DNA session, choose **DNA > Choose session...**.
- When you finish on a shared workstation, choose **DNA > Sign Out**.

## If DNA Is Not Following

1. **There is no DNA menu in Hiero.** The plugin is not installed. See the [developer guide](/integrations/hiero#installing-the-plugin).
2. **Follow In Review is off.** Switch it on at the top of DNA's sidebar.
3. **Hiero is on a different session or playlist.** The DNA menu in Hiero names the session it drives and the playlist it uses. The version has to be on that playlist, or on a playlist in that session.
4. **A version is pinned.** Unpin it in DNA.
5. **It worked, then stopped.** Choose **DNA > Sign In...** again.

If none of those fix it, the [developer guide](/integrations/hiero#troubleshooting) has a debug mode that shows what Hiero is sending.
